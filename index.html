Kuan-Kai Tung

Orthopaedic surgeon · Department of Orthopaedics, Tungs' Taichung MetroHarbor Hospital, Taichung, Taiwan

I treat fractures, joint and sports injuries, and osteoporosis, with a focus on orthopaedic trauma. My research covers two threads: radiographic and clinical outcomes of spinal fusion and disc arthroplasty, and what happens to bone health after an orthopaedic operation, including who receives osteoporosis treatment and how those drugs affect the heart and kidneys.

Much of this work uses large real-world databases, including Taiwan's National Health Insurance Research Database and international federated EHR networks, alongside single-centre cohorts from fracture liaison and spine surgery practice.

Research interests

  • Osteoporosis pharmacoepidemiology: denosumab, romosozumab, zoledronic acid
  • Secondary fracture prevention and treatment gaps after surgery
  • Geriatric hip fracture co-management and outcomes
  • Lumbar interbody fusion (ALIF, OLIF, TLIF) and cervical disc arthroplasty outcomes
  • Periprosthetic fracture fixation and arthroplasty in rheumatoid arthritis
